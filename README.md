# Picliva

**Weave moments. Make room.**

Picliva turns photos and Live Photos into one image, then helps you make room in your library. Everything happens on your iPhone: no account, no ads, no networking, and your originals are never changed.

**Live site:** <https://unlinearity.github.io/picliva-site/>

## Free, and Pro

Picliva is not a trial. Free already does the whole job of composing and reviewing, with no watermark and no export limit.

**Free** — vertical and horizontal stitching, importing, reordering, spacing, JPEG and PNG export at standard, high or full resolution, Live Photo compositions, and library review with batch deletion.

**Pro** — creative compositions with nested layouts, individual and boundary cropping, Live Photo audio, and video review in the library. One purchase, kept forever: not a subscription, nothing to cancel. The App Store shows the price for your region (US$2.99 in the United States).

## Privacy

Composition processing happens on your device. Picliva has no account, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no networking at all — your media never leaves the device, and saving always writes a new image. Library deletion is a separate action that you choose and confirm through the system. The full [privacy policy](privacy.html) spells out what the app does and does not do.
