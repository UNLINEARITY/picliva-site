Privacy policy

Picliva is developed by unlinearity. This policy describes how the app handles information when you compose photos and Live Photos, review your library, and use Picliva Pro.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version

Your photo and video processing happens on your device. There is no Picliva account, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no networking in the app. Your media is never sent to the developer.

Photos and videos

Picliva processes compositions on your device. It does not upload your photos or videos to a developer server. Photo access is controlled by Apple’s permission system and photo picker, and you can change it in system Settings. Importing, arranging and cropping a composition does not modify source media; saving creates a new result.

Library deletion is a separate action that you choose and confirm through the system. Selected media moves to Recently Deleted under Apple’s Photos rules. Media stored in iCloud may be downloaded or streamed through Apple’s system services, without being sent to the developer.

Local settings

Picliva stores preferences, the library review position, and cached Pro entitlement information locally on your device. The app does not require a Picliva account.

Purchases

Picliva Pro is an optional, non-consumable purchase handled by Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive your full payment card information. Picliva checks purchase entitlements to unlock Pro features and supports Restore Purchases.

Analytics and tracking

Picliva does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not collect personal data from the app for developer servers.

Contacting support

If you email support, the developer receives the address and information you choose to include in that email, and uses it to answer your request. Please avoid sending private photos or sensitive information unless it is needed for the issue and you choose to share it.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s practices change. The current version always shows its update date.
