Questions

The short answers, with the fine print where it belongs.

What happens when I delete?

Single mode asks for system confirmation for each item. Batch mode marks items while you review and asks for confirmation when you finish. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos, where Apple’s retention rules apply.

Does it work with Live Photos?

Yes. Put Live Photos and stills in one composition, preview the motion, and save the result as a new Live Photo. Compositions are silent by default, so keeping the sound is part of Pro.

Which iPhone, which languages?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. The interface is in English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese, and the system photo picker, permission prompts and delete confirmations follow your device language.
